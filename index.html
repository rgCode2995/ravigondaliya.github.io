A unified, secure mobile email client to organize, manage, and declutter your inbox.

About All Email

All Email is designed for Android devices to give you a clean, fast, and secure experience when managing your daily communications. Connect your Gmail, Microsoft, and custom IMAP/SMTP accounts in one centralized hub.

Key Features

  • Unified Inbox: View, search, and manage messages across multiple accounts seamlessly.
  • Quick Mailbox Actions: Mark emails as read/unread, move messages to custom folders/labels, or trash unwanted clutter in one tap.
  • Privacy-First Architecture: Your email data and access tokens remain securely cached on your local device. We never store or sell your personal email content on external servers.
  • Spam & Clutter Control: Easily organize labels, archive older threads, and keep your primary inbox clean.

Privacy & Security

All Email complies fully with the Google API Services User Data Policy, ensuring that user data accessed via Google APIs is strictly limited to user-facing email management functionalities within the app.

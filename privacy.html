1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

Personal Data

While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information ("Personal Data") used for service operation and sign-in:

  • Email address (for account setup and mailbox syncing)
  • Authentication tokens (OAuth access credentials stored locally on your device)

Usage Data & Device Diagnostics

When you access the Service via an Android mobile device, we may collect diagnostic information automatically, including the type of mobile device, unique device identifier (Android ID), IP address, operating system version, crash reports, and system performance metrics.

Cookies and Local Storage

We utilize secure on-device local databases (such as Android Room/SQLite and encrypted preferences) to cache your messages and settings offline. These are stored strictly on your local device.

2. Use of Data

All Email uses the collected data for the following specific purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the email management Service;
  • To notify you about changes and updates to our Service;
  • To enable interactive features (e.g., mail compose, search, batch label updates);
  • To provide customer care and technical support;
  • To monitor performance and detect, prevent, and resolve technical bugs.

3. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

All authentication tokens, email headers, and message cache are stored locally on your mobile device. We do not maintain an external database storing your private email content. If you log out of your account or uninstall the application, all local cache, credentials, and stored tokens are wiped from your device. You may also revoke the application's access at any time via your Google Account Permissions dashboard.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We may employ third-party SDKs and service providers to monitor app performance, serve advertisements, and analyze crashes. These third parties do NOT have access to your personal email content or Gmail data:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase & Firebase Crashlytics
  • Facebook SDK
  • OneSignal

5. Android Device Permissions

The application requires the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required to connect to mail servers and fetch mailbox data.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to detect active Wi-Fi/mobile network connections.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE: Required to optimize background data sync when connected to Wi-Fi.

6. Security of Data

The security of your data is paramount to us. We use industry-standard encryption protocols (such as TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) for all network communications and secure local storage mechanisms for authentication tokens. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
